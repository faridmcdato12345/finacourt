## Editing decisions

- Reuse the dashboard, bookings, source analytics, Booking Links, earnings, and CTA assets; no application scenes are re-recorded.
- Keep external click reporting distinct from confirmed bookings. The Booking Links claim is limited to tracked traffic sent to the configured external page.
- Keep payout language limited to available earnings and owner-requested payout. No instant, same-day, or automatic payout claim is made.
- Keep all important value statements burned into the frame so the ad remains understandable when muted.
- Keep the logo centered at X=960 on the CTA frame.
- Preserve browser-free 1920×1080 source footage and deterministic demo data.
- Use the supplied `audio-ad-2.wav` narration with a gentle 1.06× tempo adjustment so the complete recording fits without cutting words.
- Use one CTA only: “Message us for a quick demo.”

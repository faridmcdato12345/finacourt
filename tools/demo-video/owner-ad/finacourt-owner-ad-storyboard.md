# FinACourt owner ad storyboard

Target: 24 seconds, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 MP4. The ad reuses only isolated local demo footage from the existing owner-demo pipeline.

| Time | Scene | Real UI / visual | Burned message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Hook | Darkened, blurred owner-bookings UI | Still managing court reservations through Messenger? |
| 3–8s | Bookings and schedules | Owner Bookings page with booking totals and court schedule | Manage bookings and schedules in one place. |
| 8–13s | Source insight | Owner Visits & bookings source report | See where players are coming from. Know where to focus your marketing. |
| 13–18s | Booking Links | Facebook, Google, Instagram, QR, and Shared Link cards | Already using another booking platform? Keep it. Track the traffic going to it. |
| 18–24s | CTA | Fresh centered FinACourt brand frame | Message us for a quick demo. Currently no monthly subscription for court owners. finacourt.asia |

## Editing decisions

- Booking Links was selected for Scene 4 because the existing clip is cleaner and stronger than the available payout footage.
- Existing kiosk-mode 1920×1080 scene recordings contain no browser chrome. Each UI scene is cropped to a focused 9:16 slice and scaled without stretching.
- The old landscape caption area is masked before new mobile-safe captions are added.
- The hook appears on frame one; there is no logo-only intro or slow fade.
- The CTA is newly rendered at 1080×1920. The logo, FinACourt wordmark, headline, offer, CTA, and domain all use the exact X=540 center axis.
- Cuts are intentionally fast and clean. No copyrighted music or voiceover is used; the ad remains complete with sound off.

## Editing decisions

- Booking Links remains Scene 4 because its native landscape footage is clearer and more compelling than the available payout footage.
- The source recordings are already 1920×1080 kiosk-mode captures with no browser chrome, so the desktop interface is restored rather than cropped from the vertical ad.
- A restrained lower-third replaces the captions from the longer owner demo while leaving the navigation, cards, schedules, and reports understandable.
- The first frame contains both the real owner dashboard and the Messenger pain-point hook.
- The CTA is freshly rendered at 1920×1080. Its logo center is exactly X=960, Y=540; all other closing elements share X=960.
- No copyrighted music or voiceover is added. Burned captions make the ad complete with sound off.
